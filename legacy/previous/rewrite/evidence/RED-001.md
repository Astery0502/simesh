# RED-001 Evidence

## Fixed Order, Capacity Invariance, And IEEE Behavior

Eleven focused tests and all 149 accumulated rewrite tests pass. Coverage
includes exact manual slot/x/y/z order over translated subregions/fields,
nonzero initial state, empty bit preservation, persistent capacity invariance,
capacity-sensitive partial merging, alternative merge trees, read-only
finalization, FND-002 streaming zero reach, and atomic field/region/state/
writability/overlap failures.

Independent audit checked 1,500 randomized subregions and contiguous chunk
splits plus 1,000 merge/finalize cases; all matched scalar binary64 order
bitwise. Empty updates perform no state write. Negative-zero and nonfinite tests
cover `+0 + -0`, `-0 + -0`, `-0 + +0`, NaN propagation, opposite infinities,
and finite overflow followed by infinity. No correctness, ordering, atomicity,
allocation, metric, or performance defect remains.

The ordered stream `[1e16,1,-1e16,1]` yields persistent serial `1`, while two
size-two partials merged yield `0`. For leaves `[1e16,-1e16,1]`, left grouping
yields `1` and right grouping `0`. This explicitly demonstrates why the
mathematical reduction may be associative while binary merge trees are not.

For `[1e16] + [1]*1000 + [-1e16]`, serial gives `0`, NumPy on this host gives
`986`, and `math.fsum` gives `1000`. The serial absolute error is retained as a
strategy property; cancellation excludes the relative metric by the declared
`sqrt(eps)*sum(abs(values))` threshold.

## Real Memmap Composition

A read-only 257-block `2^3` memmap repeats `[1e16,1,-1e16,1]` twice per block:

| Capacity | Persistent state | Sequential chunk partials merged |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 1 | 257 |
| 7 | 1 | 37 |
| 31 | 1 | 9 |
| 257 | 1 | 1 |

Whole-memmap NumPy returns `64`; `math.fsum` returns `1028`. The persistent path
is bit-identical across every capacity because no-closure chunks continue one
global addition sequence. Partial sums intentionally expose a different
capacity-dependent strategy.

Calls retain zero traced bytes with a fixed 1,213-byte peak. NumPy is
descriptive: its faster pairwise/vector strategy produces different low bits and
cannot replace the fixed serial contract.

For a 4,096-block read-only memmap with one `8^3` field (16,777,216 bytes):

| Capacity | Chunks | Managed workspace | Runtime | Mvalue/s | GB/s | Traced peak |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |

All sums have the same bits and exact analytic value `4,829,741,056`; the runs
incur at most two minor and zero major faults after warm setup. Direct NumPy
takes 0.462 ms but uses its different reduction order. The serial addition
dependency is the limiting cost; vector, compensated, and parallel variants
would change state/order semantics and are not retained.
