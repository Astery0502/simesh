It owns only COARSER primary phases, FINER source phases, relative levels,
reduced-coordinate/phase compatibility, mask neutrality, canonical source
order, and exact `255` sentinels.  TGT boxes, REL actions, PBC narrowing,
source/workspace geometry, RST/PRL values, payload, and scheduling remain
independent.  The five-question result is Green.

fixture satisfy that rule, including tangential high phases and mixed masks.
FINER count/sign/order semantics pass exhaustive direction/mask checks.
