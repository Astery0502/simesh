# TOP-001 Evidence

## Correctness

Eleven focused tests and all 47 accumulated rewrite tests pass. The Cython
six-face table agrees exactly with the independent coordinate reference for all
216 positive shapes with extents from one through six. Golden nonsymmetric and
singleton cases freeze face order, zero-based IDs, and the `-1` sentinel.
Boundary counts, reciprocity, exact displacements, non-self neighbors,
singleton faces, and commuting orthogonal steps are checked exhaustively.

Five retained current-implementation comparisons cover singleton, cubic, and
asymmetric grids. After extracting current columns `(12,14,10,16,4,22)` and
subtracting one from its one-based IDs, every face entry agrees exactly;
current type one occurs exactly at rewrite sentinel entries and type three at
every same-level face. A composed test reconstructs all 26 edge/corner entries
from face steps in every axis order and also checks current center ID/type;
together these reproduce the complete current 27-slot table exactly.

Malformed inverse coordinates, forward maps, shapes, dtypes, contiguity,
writability, overlap, nonpositive extents, and volume overflow are rejected
before output mutation. Read-only MOR maps are accepted and preserved. An
independent safety review found no remaining issue after the explicit center
regression was added.

## Runtime, Throughput, And Memory

Final Apple M1 measurements used 15 repetitions of the checked caller-buffer
operation, including structural map validation.

| Shape | Blocks | Checked time | Mblocks/s | Mfaces/s | Face bytes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `(16,16,16)` | 4,096 | 27.0 us | 151.9 | 911.6 | 196,608 |
| `(32,32,32)` | 32,768 | 163.4 us | 200.5 | 1,203.1 | 1,572,864 |
| `(64,64,64)` | 262,144 | 1.357 ms | 193.2 | 1,159.3 | 12,582,912 |
| `(31,29,27)` | 24,273 | 116.7 us | 208.0 | 1,247.9 | 1,165,104 |
| `(127,113,97)` | 1,392,047 | 8.755 ms | 159.0 | 954.0 | 66,818,256 |
| `(65536,1,1)` | 65,536 | 230.0 us | 284.9 | 1,709.6 | 3,145,728 |

The largest case spends 3.52 ms validating the maps and 5.07 ms filling faces;
both passes scale linearly and allocate no scratch. `tracemalloc` reports a
fixed 1,093-byte peak and 64-byte retained increase across all sizes. Required
storage is exactly `48*N` bytes. A full 27-direction `int64` table would use
`216*N` bytes: 300,682,152 bytes instead of 66,818,256 in the largest case.

The validated memoryview baseline took 20.42 ms for `(127,113,97)`. Raw pointers
inside the unchanged checked boundary reduced this to 8.75 ms (2.3x); its fill
pass alone improved from 16.21 to 5.07 ms (3.2x). Fusing validation with fill
would violate no-mutation-on-invalid behavior, and cached diagonal tables would
trade 4.5x storage for a benefit that can only be assessed with HAL-002 payload
movement. Those variants are therefore stopped or deferred at the correct
consumer.

