# REL-001 Evidence

## Correctness And Decomposition

REL-001 materializes selected balanced direction records only: kind,
physical-component mask, exact touching source count, and canonical global
source leaf IDs.  TOP-002 still owns raw contact discovery, BAL-001 owns
admissibility, PBC/HAL own physical coordinate/value transforms, STO owns
support/capacity, and transfer capabilities own cell phases and values.
Independent contract and implementation reviews found no remaining issue.

Thirty-nine focused tests and all 316 accumulated rewrite tests pass.  Tests
cover level-one reduction to HPL source/mask meaning; all 26 directions and
fine child phases; every kind, count, sentinel, and reciprocal relation; pure
and mixed physical masks; reordered/repeated/empty selections; maximum level
63; atomic selector/direction/output/alias errors; upstream rejection of an
unbalanced forest; current supported records; and real refined face evidence.

The common-lattice reference uses Python-integer leaf boxes and never calls
TOP/MOR or follows child pointers.  Production agrees exactly for 20 randomized
balanced test forests; a separate development probe covered 14,378 records over
55 forests.  A mixed physical/finer case freezes the decomposition decision:
the physical component is neutralized, both child phases are retained, and any
boundary-mode/reach-specific narrowing remains downstream.

## Real Metadata And Current Behavior

The balanced WENO tree produces 587,964 records over all 26 directions:

| Kind | Records |
| --- | ---: |
| physical | 3,602 |
| coarser | 86,186 |
| same | 480,114 |

There are 603,468 emitted source occurrences, 30,386 records with a nonzero
physical mask, and 26,784 mixed physical/nonphysical records.  Mixed records
retain 3,274 coarser, 22,048 same, and 1,462 finer relations.  Current agrees
on every unmasked and pure-physical supported kind but reports all 26,784 mixed
records as physical, losing their in-domain source.  REL-001 intentionally
preserves that source, matching the already established HPL composition rule.

Current balanced relation identity and fine expansion otherwise agree with the
rewrite.  Known gap-two cases are rejected by BAL-001 before REL; current would
misclassify some as physical or place internal-child zeroes in its support
table.  The real header is staggered, so these results remain relation metadata
evidence rather than payload support.

On the real tree, checked REL takes `8.078 ms` at `72.78 million records/s`;
the scratch-free fused fill is `8.003 ms`, allocating output is `9.157 ms`, and
the full FST-to-BAL-to-REL lifecycle is `15.372 ms`.  Current forest plus eager
connectivity construction is `18.597 ms` and retains 10,673,808 bytes.  Copying
those already cached current tables is `0.517 ms`.

The full REL result is 20,578,740 caller-owned bytes, intentionally larger than
the current retained cache because it contains four complete selected source
slots and mixed masks.  A 1,024-leaf bounded traversal reuses exactly 931,840
bytes and completes in `8.218 ms`, preserving throughput without retained
leaf-sized state.  Checked calls observe a fixed 2,728--2,792-byte traced peak
and 120--184 retained traced bytes across the scaling range.

## TOP-003 Decision

Six face directions take `1.779 ms`; the remaining 20 edge/corner directions
take `6.228 ms`.  After physical reduction, only 145,950 records, 24.8% of the
full workload, are face contacts.  A candidate TOP-003 cache would retain
1,221,156 bytes on this tree, and its raw face-contact construction has a
`1.552 ms` lower bound before kind/cache writes.

Even an ideal zero-cost cached lookup cannot amortize construction on the first
face pass and cannot accelerate the other three quarters of REL records.  No
concrete consumer yet requires repeated full face reuse, so TOP-003 remains
deferred.  The later transfer/halo composition may reopen it with actual cache
build, lookup, total managed memory, and reuse evidence.

Fused on-demand REL avoids the two-pass alternative's 4,703,712-byte raw target
scratch and already spends essentially all measured time in required lookup and
output work.  Together with bounded throughput, no further credible current
variant materially improves the runtime-memory-complexity trade-off.
