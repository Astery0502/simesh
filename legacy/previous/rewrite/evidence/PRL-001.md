## Boundary, Intentional Difference, And Correctness

PRL-001 owns one Cartesian 3D ratio-two transformation: mathematical floor
mapping from explicit aligned origins, exact phase `+/-0.25`, the seven-cell
axial coarse cross, three shared LIM slopes, x/y/z products and ordered
additions, coarse-valid reach, and explicit fine-box mutation.  Relations,
source slots, target selection, coarse-workspace assembly/lifetime, RST
placement, physical rules, backend, and scheduling remain outside.  The
five-question result is Green; the stable contract and its LIM-reuse
clarification passed independent review before optimization.

Exact integer phase intentionally removes current physical-coordinate eta
rounding.  WENO metadata checks all 814,104 leaf/axis/index mappings: coarse
indices never differ, while current has 27 eta bit patterns and reaches
`2.842170943040401e-14` absolute deviation from exact phase.  Production and
the exact-phase reference are bitwise gates.  A zero-origin dyadic current face
is also bitwise equal; a non-dyadic face satisfies the contracted local
slope-weighted eta plus six-operation roundoff bound.

Thirteen focused tests cover negative/positive offsets and asymmetric origins;
all phases; exact six-face reach and diagonal poison; constants, affine child
placement/average recovery, step/quadratic monotonicity; partial translated odd
boxes; random multi-slot/field bitwise reference comparison; signed-zero and
nonfinite x/y/z order; empty/read-only/exterior preservation; complete
validation/overflow/overlap atomicity; RST workspace assembly; slot-order
substitution; current dyadic/non-dyadic comparisons; and WENO mapping-only
evidence.  Fine- and coarse-centric implementations produce the same complete
arrays bitwise.

Independent implementation review approved floor/overflow safety, validation

## Loop Optimization, Scaling, And Memory

Correctness was frozen with the fine-output-centric loop closest to current.
The retained coarse-center-centric variant evaluates three pure LIM slopes once
and reuses their bits for up to eight children, while every child still performs
all three phase products and x/y/z additions.  It introduces no slope array or
scratch.

Final Apple arm64 standard workload
`coarse float64[64,4,10,10,10] -> fine float64[64,4,16,16,16]`:

| Path | Runtime ms | M fine field-cells/s | Effective GB/s |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Checked coarse-centric | 4.706 | 222.8 | 14.26 |
| Unchecked coarse-centric | 4.650 | 225.5 | 14.43 |
| Unchecked fine-centric | 11.752 | 89.2 | 5.71 |

Coarse-centric reuse is 2.53x faster; checked overhead is 1.2%.  All three full
8,388,608-byte outputs are bitwise equal before and after interleaved timing.
A checked call retains zero traced bytes and peaks at 1,312 bytes.  All eight
phases occur exactly 512 spatial times each.

## Immediate Composition And Current Evidence

The composed benchmark starts with an explicitly preassembled coarse workspace,
overwrites its self interior through RST, then invokes PRL.  It contains no
relations, IDs, masks, target-halo policy, or physical scheduling.  Resident
assembly/RST/PRL times are 0.051/0.254/4.731 ms.  Capacities 1/8/64 use 64/8/1
batches and reproduce the complete resident output bitwise:

| Capacity | Coarse/fine workspace | PRL ms | M cells/s | Total ms |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 32 / 128 KiB | 5.311 | 197.5 | 6.202 |
| 8 | 256 / 1,024 KiB | 5.007 | 209.4 | 5.557 |
| 64 | 2,048 / 8,192 KiB | 4.894 | 214.2 | 5.355 |

PRL dominates this simple composed path, so slope reuse is materially useful.
The current aggregate ghost call is not a direct runtime comparator.  Its
dyadic face is bitwise exact.  The non-dyadic face has maximum eta delta
`4.996e-16`, output absolute/relative differences `3.331e-16`/`1.354e-15`,
maximum 12 ULP, and maximum contracted bound `3.450e-15`.

Coarse-centric reuse is exact and decisively improves the only credible serial
variant.  A materialized slope array would add 24 bytes per coarse field-cell,
while no present consumer reuses slopes beyond the eight children already
covered inline.  Parallelism, transfer fusion, and cached slopes therefore have
no current runtime-memory-complexity justification; optimization stops here.
