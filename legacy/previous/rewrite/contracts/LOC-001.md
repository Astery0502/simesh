# LOC-001 Contract: Exact Cartesian 3D Refined Point Ownership

## Responsibility

LOC-001 maps explicit finite Cartesian points to their canonical refined leaf
owners under one exact face/tie rule. It defines no cell owner, interpolation,
payload, halo, reader, cache, point grouping, adjacency fast path, streamline
step, or termination policy.

## Stable Operation

```text
fill_refined_point_leaf_ids(
    domain_lower,
    domain_upper,
    root_shape,
    domain_cell_counts,
    block_cell_counts,
    max_level,
    coord_to_rank,
    root_node_ids,
    child_node_ids,
    node_leaf_ids,
    points,
    point_leaf_ids,
) -> None
```

- Domain bounds are borrowed finite C-contiguous native `float64[3]` with
  strictly increasing components.
- Root/domain/block counts are borrowed C-contiguous `int64[3]`, positive, and
  satisfy `domain_cell_counts == root_shape * block_cell_counts` without signed
  overflow.
- `max_level` is the exact positive Python integer returned by FST-002 for the
  unchanged artifact lifecycle.
- `coord_to_rank`, `root_node_ids`, `child_node_ids`, and `node_leaf_ids` are
  the borrowed read-only C-contiguous `int64[root_shape]`, `int64[R]`,
  `int64[K,8]`, and `int64[K]` arrays from that lifecycle. LOC validates the
  shapes needed for safe access but does not repeat MOR/FST semantic
  conformance.
- The same lifecycle has passed GEO-002 for every leaf under the supplied
  domain/count metadata. This proves all descended leaf bounds and spacings are
  representable. Mutation of any participating artifact starts a new lifecycle.
- `points` is borrowed read-only C-contiguous finite `float64[P,3]`. Empty,
  repeated, and arbitrarily ordered points are accepted.
- `point_leaf_ids` is caller-owned writable C-contiguous `int64[P]` and may not
  overlap any input.

All validation completes before output mutation. Success overwrites every
output entry, mutates no input, and retains nothing. The allocating
`refined_point_leaf_ids(...)` helper returns the same array.

## Domain And Tie Semantics

The physical query domain is half-open on every axis:

```text
domain_lower[a] <= point[a] < domain_upper[a]
```

A finite point outside on any axis, including exact equality with any global
upper endpoint, receives leaf ID `-1`. The lower endpoint is owned. Any NaN or
infinity anywhere is an invalid query and rejects the whole call rather than
being classified as physical exterior.

For level `ell`, axis `a`, and global cell-face index `g`, use GEO's operation
order:

```text
base_h[a] = (domain_upper[a] - domain_lower[a])
            / float(domain_cell_counts[a])
h[a]      = ldexp(base_h[a], -(ell - 1))
N[a]      = domain_cell_counts[a] * 2**(ell - 1)

face(0)    = domain_lower[a]
face(N[a]) = domain_upper[a]
face(g)    = domain_lower[a] + float(g) * h[a] otherwise
```

Conversion, multiplication, and addition are separate binary64 operations;
accumulated stepping and fused multiply-add are forbidden. At level one, the
root coordinate is the canonical native-cell owner divided by the block cell
count. The native-cell owner is the greatest `g` in `[0,N-1]` whose canonical
lower face is no greater than the point, exactly SAM-002's highest-face rule.
Thus coincident representable root/block faces choose the highest valid side.

At an internal node of level `ell` and coordinate `c`, the child midpoint on
axis `a` is the level-`ell+1` face with index

```text
(2*c[a] + 1) * block_cell_counts[a].
```

The child bit is one exactly when that face is no greater than the point. Child
column is `xbit + 2*ybit + 4*zbit`. Equality at every internal face therefore
chooses the positive/upper child independently per axis; face, edge, corner,
and coarse/fine ties all have one deterministic owner.

## Validation, Errors, And Atomicity

Exact ndarray type, dtype, rank, shape, C-contiguity, output writability, and
nonoverlap are checked before unchecked access. Domain/count compatibility,
root volume, exact `max_level`, deepest effective cell counts, and base/deepest
normal positive spacing are also checked before mutation. Type/dtype and
noninteger scalar failures raise `TypeError`; invalid layouts, domain, counts,
nonfinite points, or lifecycle shapes raise `ValueError`; signed integer
overflow raises `OverflowError`.

MOR/FST/GEO semantic corruption after their established lifecycle is a caller
contract violation. LOC neither rescans all nodes/leaves nor constructs a
visited set on every point batch.

## Independent Evidence And Exactness

Leaf IDs and exterior sentinels compare exactly. The independent reference
materializes canonical GEO-002 bounds for every leaf and scans them under the
same half-open inequalities; it does not descend child pointers. Evidence
covers nonsymmetric roots, mixed depths and all child phases, all leaf lower/
upper faces and their `nextafter` neighbors, root and coarse/fine face/edge/
corner ties, repeated/reordered/exterior points, depths one and the accepted
maximum, and level-one reduction to SAM-002 owner IDs.

The historical archived locator is comparison evidence only: it combines
closed overlapping leaf boxes with first-match order and mishandles no-match
and exact global-upper cases. Those behaviors are intentionally not preserved.

A closed-upper proposal was independently rejected. For the GEO-valid x-domain
`[274877906944.0,274877906944.1]`, 26 roots, block extent 32, and a refined
final root, the last level-two leaf has
`h=6.008148193359375e-05` and lower face `274877906944.098`. At exact upper,
SAM-003 arithmetic gives `u=32.007936507936506`, hence `j0=32`, beyond the
frozen one-cell stencil. Half-open location requires neither a new clamp nor a
future periodic endpoint exception.

## Performance And Memory

The production operation verifies a normal root candidate against canonical
faces and falls back to binary search only when necessary, then follows one
flat child per level. Its ordinary cost is `O(P*depth)` and worst-case cost is
`O(P*(sum(log(root_shape)) + depth))`, with fixed scalar scratch per point. No
leaf-, node-, depth-, or point-sized temporary is allocated; caller output is
exactly `8*P` bytes.

LOC-001 is a `hot-kernel` capability. Measure single-point latency, batch
points/s, root/depth scaling, coherent and scattered query order, interior and
exterior mixes, fixed allocation, and immediate zero/trilinear point
composition. A retained BVH/hash, maximum-level grid, last-leaf/neighbor walk,
or parallel traversal requires a measured later consumer trigger.

## Immediate Composition

FST-002 and GEO-002 establish the immutable forest/geometry lifecycle.
SAM-004/SAM-005 consume exact leaf IDs through explicit slot/group routing.
RPS-001 locates once before grouping. A later streamline executor may test a
last leaf and exact neighbor relation before falling back to LOC-001, but those
stateful execution choices do not change this owner contract.
