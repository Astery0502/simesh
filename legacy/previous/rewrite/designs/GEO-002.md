# GEO-002 Design: Selected Refined Leaf Geometry

## Problem

Refined consumers need physical bounds and cell spacing for explicit canonical
leaf IDs.  The current mesh computes the right mathematical quantities but
retains a mutable `float64[L,9]` table, repeats spacing for every leaf, and forms
upper faces by adding a rounded width to a rounded lower face.  Equivalent
same-level and coarse/fine faces can therefore differ by one ulp.

## Alternatives

Preserving current `rnode` arithmetic would retain its face gaps and full-table
ownership.  Materializing all faces on one maximum-level lattice would make
identity obvious but couples cost and representability to the deepest leaf.
Computing floating bounds from local accumulated widths has the same drift as
the current path.

Use selected canonical leaf IDs, one level-specific integer cell-face index,
and a dyadic scaling of the GEO-001 base spacing.  Snap global endpoints to the
supplied domain bounds.  Bounds and per-slot spacing are filled atomically into
caller buffers; no full table or center array is retained.

## Decomposition Record

Responsibility: map selected Cartesian 3D FST leaf identities to physical
bounds and per-leaf cell spacing under one canonical affine/dyadic rule.

Owned semantic decisions: explicit domain/count compatibility, selected leaf
order/repetition, level-specific integer cell-face identity, dyadic spacing,
endpoint snapping, selected numerical representability, output ownership, and
atomicity.

Explicitly non-owned decisions: FST construction/conformance, topology,
balance, contact/cache representation, cell centers, owner/tie rules,
sampling/interpolation, payloads, halos, storage, chunks, backends, scheduling,
metadata inference, periodicity, and even-block transfer constraints.

Inputs and outputs: borrowed domain/count and FST-subset arrays plus explicit
leaf IDs; caller-owned `float64[S,2,3]` bounds and `float64[S,3]` spacing.

Mutation and ownership: inputs remain unchanged, outputs are fully overwritten
only after complete validation, and nothing is retained.

Access pattern / halo reach: selected metadata rows only; no payload access or
halo reach.

Independent reference or invariant: exact `Fraction` domain geometry; bitwise
GEO-001 reduction at level one; bit-identical same/coarse/fine shared faces;
exact supplied endpoints; selected overflow/collapse cases; current `rnode`
and real refined-tree comparison.

Immediate producer and consumer: FST-002 establishes the unchanged artifact
lifecycle; later refined relation/transfer and sampling capabilities consume
plain bounds and spacing.  GEO-002 does not require TOP-002 or BAL-001.

Kernel and composed measurement boundary: selected leaves/s, level/selection
scaling, fixed allocation, exact `72*S` output bytes, FST-to-geometry lifecycle,
real-tree time, and current cached-table comparison.

Five-question result: yes/yes/yes/yes/yes.  Bounds and spacing are two atomic
outputs of one geometry transform; splitting them would duplicate validation
and allow incompatible rounding.

## Performance Gate

Measure sparse selections and full uniform/adaptive forests.  Runtime must be
linear in selected leaves, allocation must remain fixed apart from caller
outputs, and composed FST-to-geometry work must remain small relative to current
forest/mesh construction.  A full retained geometry cache is not introduced
without a later consumer-specific reuse measurement.
