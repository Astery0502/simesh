# RPS-001 Contract: Bounded Repeated Refined Point-Sampling Execution

## Responsibility

RPS-001 locates one explicit point request, stable-groups repeated leaf owners,
and schedules bounded functional block reads for the already contracted
SAM-004 zero-order or SAM-005 trilinear numerical path. It owns call-scoped
grouping and workspace scheduling, not leaf/cell ownership, interpolation,
halo values, backend implementation, persistent caching, prefetch, neighbor
transition, parallel policy, stepping, or termination.

## Stable Operations

```text
execute_refined_zero_order_points_from_blocks(
    reader,
    points,
    field_ids,
    domain_lower,
    domain_upper,
    root_shape,
    domain_cell_counts,
    block_cell_counts,
    max_level,
    coord_to_rank,
    root_node_ids,
    node_levels,
    node_coords,
    child_node_ids,
    node_leaf_ids,
    leaf_node_ids,
    slot_capacity,
    point_values,
) -> RepeatedPointExecutionStats

execute_refined_trilinear_points_from_blocks(
    reader,
    points,
    field_ids,
    domain_lower,
    domain_upper,
    root_shape,
    domain_cell_counts,
    block_cell_counts,
    max_level,
    coord_to_rank,
    root_node_ids,
    node_levels,
    node_coords,
    child_node_ids,
    node_leaf_ids,
    leaf_node_ids,
    boundary_modes,
    normal_field_slots,
    slot_capacity,
    point_values,
) -> RepeatedPointExecutionStats
```

`reader` is a STO-003 functional `BlockReader` with shape
`(L,Fsource,Bx,By,Bz)`. `field_ids` is borrowed C-contiguous `int64[F]`;
arbitrary order and repeats are retained as output columns. Points and all
domain/MOR/FST/GEO metadata have LOC-001 representations from one unchanged
lifecycle. `point_values` is caller-owned writable C-contiguous
`float64[P,F]` and may not overlap reader memory arrays or any metadata.

Finite exterior points, including exact global upper endpoints, are retained
in the plan with owner `-1` but their output rows are bitwise preserved. Any
nonfinite point rejects the complete request before output mutation or I/O.

For a nonempty interior request, `slot_capacity` is a positive exact integer no
larger than `L`. In the zero-order operation it bounds directly resident owner
interiors. In the trilinear operation it is RHE-001's primary/support slot
capacity and must satisfy its `min(57,L)` lower bound. Empty or all-exterior
requests accept zero capacity and perform no nonempty read. The two entrypoints
are intentionally separate: zero order does not inherit balance, refined-halo,
even-`B`, `B>=4`, PBC, or all-26 support requirements. Trilinear execution
inherits RHE-001's balanced Cartesian 3D, even `B>=4`, one-cell side-reach
scope and boundary configuration.

## Deterministic Point Plan

LOC-001 runs exactly once over the full point array. Let `I` be the number of
inside points and `U` the number of distinct nonnegative owners. RPS constructs
call-scoped arrays:

```text
point_owner_leaf_ids int64[P]
grouped_point_indices int64[I]
owner_leaf_ids int64[U]
owner_offsets int64[U+1]
```

`owner_leaf_ids` is strictly ascending. Point indices are grouped by that
owner; original input order is stable inside every group. Offsets delimit the
groups and span `[0,I]`. Sampling writes final `point_values` rows by original
point index, so capacity does not change point or field order. Repeated point
coordinates remain distinct rows.

The plan requires `O(P+I+U)` explicit memory and is not claimed bounded
independently of the caller's point/result request. Payload and halo workspace
is `O(slot_capacity)`. No point-value staging buffer or separate point capacity
is required because grouped kernels write final rows directly, including an
arbitrarily large one-owner group.

## Zero-Order Execution

Process ascending owner groups in batches of at most `slot_capacity`. For each
batch, call the reader once for exactly the batch's unique owner leaf IDs and
requested fields, reading the complete interiors into a reusable canonical
`float64[K,F,Bx,By,Bz]` workspace. Then call the unchecked SAM-004 group kernel
once and reuse the workspace. No support leaf or halo cell is requested.

Every unique owner is read exactly once. Thus nonempty reader calls and sampler
calls equal `ceil(U/slot_capacity)`, and selected load count equals `U`.

## Trilinear Execution And Private RHE Boundary

Pass the complete ascending `owner_leaf_ids` selection to the RHE-001 chunk
runner with lower/upper halo `(1,1,1)`. RHE plans and reads all-26 support and
completes every primary workspace under its existing numerical contracts.

Its public writer operation is unchanged. Internally, RHE may parameterize its
terminal action with a private synchronous completed-primary consumer. RPS's
consumer is invoked exactly once for each nonempty fully completed primary
prefix, after all halo actions and before workspace reuse or the next read. It
receives the exact input-primary offset, ascending primary IDs, and the full
padded valid box; support-only rows are excluded. It calls only the unchecked
SAM-005 kernel into final result rows, returns `None`, and neither mutates nor
retains any workspace view. Consumer exceptions stop immediately under the
same completed-prefix failure semantics.

This private hook adds no fifth stable capability. It must not change public
RHE's empty writer conformance call, writer IDs/call order, alias checks,
managed-byte accounting, returned stats, values, or failure behavior. If the
hook becomes public/substitutable or gains another independent consumer, its
protocol requires a separate capability review.

## Validation, I/O Failure, And Atomicity

Before the first nonempty reader call or any output mutation, RPS validates:

- descriptor shape/callable/aliases, fields, output, and capacity;
- all LOC domain/count/MOR/FST/GEO representations and every point;
- the complete deterministic owner/group plan;
- every owner-level zero cell or trilinear stencil and workspace byte bound;
- for trilinear, the complete RHE boundary/balance/lifecycle and PBC metadata.

The zero path makes the STO-003 empty reader conformance call after preflight;
the trilinear path retains RHE's empty reader call. Ordinary contract errors
therefore leave output unchanged and make no nonempty read. Type/dtype and
scalar-kind failures raise `TypeError`; invalid geometry, layout, ownership,
mapping, capacity, stencil, boundary metadata, or overlap raises `ValueError`;
integer/byte overflow raises `OverflowError`.

After preflight, an external reader failure may leave rows belonging to earlier
completed ascending owner groups written. Those rows need not form an input-
order prefix. RPS provides no transaction over an arbitrary backend. Mutation
of an already validated lifecycle is a caller violation.

## Statistics

Success returns exact Python integers:

```text
RepeatedPointExecutionStats(
    point_count,
    inside_point_count,
    owner_count,
    chunk_count,
    reader_call_count,
    sampler_call_count,
    selected_load_count,
    maximum_selected_slots,
    managed_array_bytes,
)
```

Counts exclude empty conformance calls. `selected_load_count` is `U` for zero
order and includes RHE support loads for trilinear. `managed_array_bytes` is
the exact sum of live caller-independent NumPy plan and payload/RHE workspace
arrays at peak logical execution; caller points/output, immutable metadata,
backend page cache, allocator internals, and process RSS are reported
separately. Sorting-library transient peak is measured rather than hidden in
the exact array sum.

## Correctness And Performance Evidence

The resident comparator first locates/groups identically, then samples complete
canonical leaf arrays. Bounded results must be bitwise equal for zero order and
use SAM-005's numerical metrics for trilinear across minimum, intermediate,
and full capacities, array and non-array counting readers, clustered and
scattered owners, repeated points/fields, every refined relation and PBC kind,
empty/all-exterior requests, and injected first/later reader failures. Tests
also freeze exact reader IDs/calls/bytes, one production per unique primary,
support loads, output preservation, and unchanged public RHE behavior after
the private runner extraction.

RPS-001 is `composition-only`. The group standard benchmark records locate,
plan, read, halo, and sample stages; time to first result; point and field-value
throughput; `P`, `I`, `U`, and owner reuse `I-U`; requested/read/support/output
bytes and amplification; calls/chunks; capacities; managed plan/workspace
bytes; traced peak and RSS; cold/warm behavior; and resident/bounded/current
comparisons where meanings agree. Repeated public RHE preflight, all-26 support,
sorting, a last-leaf/neighbor path, point-plan streaming, or persistent cache is
reopened only when this or the later native-reader streamline profile makes it
material.

## Immediate Consumer

The next native selective `.dat` adapter substitutes for the reader without
changing RPS. The later streamline vertical slice may initially issue bounded
point batches through this contract, then introduce explicit last-leaf,
neighbor-transition, and persistent-cache policies under separate evidence.
