# CWA-001 Contract: Apply A COARSER Workspace Plan

## Responsibility

CWA-001 applies one CSP-001 plan to assemble the complete rectangular coarse
workspace required by one CWP-001 row.  It composes exact copy, RST-001, and
PWA-001 semantics without changing them.  It defines no relation/support
planning, PRL value, workspace allocation/lifetime, storage callback, chunk
policy, final primary mutation, periodicity, or 2D behavior.

## Stable Operation

```text
apply_coarser_workspace_plan(
    selected_payload, coarse_workspace,
    interior_lower, interior_upper,
    workspace_required_lower, workspace_required_upper,
    transfer_count, record_count,
    plan_source_slots, plan_source_is_fine, plan_physical_masks,
    plan_directions,
    plan_source_lower, plan_source_upper,
    plan_base_lower, plan_base_upper,
    plan_target_lower, plan_target_upper,
    plan_logical_interior_lower, plan_logical_interior_upper,
    plan_storage_logical_offsets,
    boundary_modes, normal_field_slots,
) -> None
```

`selected_payload` is borrowed C-contiguous `float64[S,F,bx,by,bz]` with valid
interiors.  `coarse_workspace` is caller-owned writable C-contiguous
`float64[1,F,cx,cy,cz]`, nonoverlapping with selected payload and metadata.
Interior and required bounds are `int64[3]`; the required box is contained in
the workspace.  Mode/normal metadata has the PBC/HAL representation.

Plan arrays have the exact fixed 18-row CSP shapes and sentinel representation.
`0 <= transfer_count <= record_count <= 18`.  Transfer records have mask zero,
valid source slot, `source_is_fine` zero or one, zero logical metadata, and
base equal target.  Physical records have nonzero mask, slot `-1`, flag `255`,
zero source boxes, PWA metadata, and a base contained in at least one completed
transfer target.  The inactive suffix has CSP's exact sentinels.

All active target boxes are nonempty, pairwise disjoint, contained in required,
and their mathematical volumes sum to the required volume.  This proves exact
rectangular coverage.  All physical targets are disjoint from all bases.

## Exact Application And Validity

In transfer-prefix order:

- flag zero copies the source box bitwise into the equal-extent target;
- flag one applies RST-001's exact eight-value addition/multiply rule from the
  source box into the half-extent target.

Targets are disjoint, so transfer order is not numerical.  After every transfer
base is valid, apply PWA once to the physical suffix using its plan-provided
logical interiors/offsets, directions, masks, bases, targets, and boundary data.
On success every cell in `workspace_required` is valid, its exterior is bitwise
preserved, and selected payload/metadata are unchanged.  The workspace may be
reused without clearing because the complete required rectangle is overwritten.

## Validation, Errors, And Cost

Validate all representations, counts/sentinels/order, slot/field extents,
source bounds/ratios, required target partition, PWA images and RAW/WAW safety,
workspace/input aliasing, modes/normals, and all derived arithmetic before the
first workspace write.  Wrong types/dtypes/scalars raise `TypeError`; invalid
shape/layout/range/plan/coverage/alias raises `ValueError`; fixed-width overflow
raises `OverflowError`.  Every ordinary failure preserves the complete
workspace.

Evidence compares with an independent copy/RST/PWA composition across center,
SAME, COARSER, pure/mixed physical tiles, all COARSER direction classes and
phases, poisoned scratch, reordered fields, empty fields, nonfinite bits,
outside preservation, and atomic plan corruption.  Immediate PRL output agrees
with its independent reference and safe current dyadic cases.

CWA is composition-only.  It allocates and retains no payload-sized data; plan
storage and the caller workspace are counted by RHE.  Measure direct/RST/PWA
field-cells, complete workspace/PRL time, and composed selected-halo runtime
rather than artificial repeated orchestration.

## Immediate Composition

CWP supplies required/origin geometry and CSP supplies the complete plan.  RHE
reuses one workspace, calls CWA, then invokes PRL with CWP's exact required box
and origin.
