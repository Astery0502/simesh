# RHE-001 Contract: Bounded Selected Refined Halo Executor

## Responsibility

RHE-001 executes a complete nonperiodic Cartesian 3D refined halo for an
explicit strictly increasing primary-leaf selection through STO-003 reader and
writer descriptors.  It owns bounded chunk scheduling, temporary lifetime,
stage order, and accounting.  It introduces no topology, support, transfer,
physical, numerical, storage, sampling, operator, cache, periodic, or 2D
semantics.

## Stable Operation

```text
execute_selected_refined_halos_from_blocks(
    reader, writer,
    primary_leaf_ids, field_ids,
    root_shape, coord_to_rank, root_node_ids,
    node_levels, node_coords, child_node_ids,
    node_leaf_ids, leaf_node_ids,
    lower_halo, upper_halo,
    boundary_modes, normal_field_slots,
    slot_capacity,
) -> RefinedHaloExecutionStats
```

Forest arrays belong to one unchanged successful FST-002/BAL-001 lifecycle and
have the REL/RPH representations.  `primary_leaf_ids int64[P]` is borrowed,
C-contiguous, strictly increasing, and in range.  `field_ids int64[F]` uses
reader field positions; order and repeats are preserved.  Reader shape is
`(L,NF,Bx,By,Bz)`.

Every `B` is positive even and at least four.  Lower/upper halo triplets are
nonnegative and each entry is at most the corresponding `B/2`.  Define
`padded=B+lower+upper`, interior `[lower,lower+B)`, and requested box
`[0,padded)`.  Writer shape is `(L,F,*padded)`; its field positions are local
`0..F-1` in requested `field_ids` order.  Boundary modes have shape `(F,6)` and
normal slots refer to local field positions.

For nonempty `P`, `slot_capacity` is at least `min(57,L)` and at most `L`.
This conservative all-26 balanced bound guarantees SPR progress without a
second complete relation traversal.  Empty `P` permits zero capacity.
`slot_capacity` accepts Python/NumPy integer scalars except booleans and
rank-zero arrays, is nonnegative, and fits signed int64; for empty `P` any value
in `[0,L]` is valid.

## Exact Chunk Execution

Materialize TGT once for the 26 canonical x-fast noncenter directions.  For
each remaining candidate window of at most capacity:

1. generate exact REL rows and use SPR to accept the maximal primary prefix and
   its unique support;
2. resolve RSL slots and RPH phases;
3. before the reader, validate every actual SAME SLB/copy box, every FINER
   FRP/RST source/target, every COARSER CWP/CSP/CWA/PRL box and reach, and the
   complete primary PWA batch for the accepted chunk;
4. invoke the reader once for every selected block interior and requested field;
5. fill every unmasked base target: SAME by SLB/copy, FINER by FRP/RST, and
   COARSER by CWP/CSP/CWA followed by PRL;
6. after all bases are complete, apply PWA to every pure/mixed physical target;
7. invoke the writer once for the primary prefix and complete padded box using
   local writer field IDs `0..F-1`.

The production preflight may consume exact internal-producer invariants instead
of re-invoking every standalone checked value wrapper.  It must still, before
the reader, guard relation counts and every active source slot before unchecked
RPH; validate SAME with checked SLB and FINER with checked FRP; validate COARSER
with checked CWP and checked CSP (or the equivalent owned CSP preflight in
HPR-001), the CSP physical suffix with PWA, and mapped reach with a
zero-field checked PRL; and validate the complete primary PWA batch.  Checked
CSP already proves CWA source ratios, target partition, base ownership, and
sentinels for executor-owned nonaliasing buffers, so repeating full CWA and
zero-field copy/RST validation is not required.  A complete checked preflight
reference is retained for equivalence and performance evidence.

Full-direction target boxes are disjoint.  A physical row's base is the TGT box
for its mask-reduced direction, or the primary interior for pure physical.
FINER phases complete their whole reduced base before PWA.  Support slots retain
only reader-filled interiors; their halos are never read or mutated.  Primary
interiors are bitwise preserved and every requested primary halo cell is valid.

Advancing by SPR primary count visits each requested primary exactly once and
never writes a support-only or intervening leaf.  Capacity changes chunking and
repeated support loads, not primary output bits.

## Preflight, Failure, And Ownership

Before nonempty I/O validate all caller-controlled scalar/array/descriptor
representations, shapes/ranges/order, forest operational compatibility,
selection, fields, extent/reach/capacity, boundary modes/normals, writer shape,
and exposed reader/writer memory aliases.  Invoke one empty reader and writer
request to establish adapter conformance.  Ordinary request errors occur before
the first nonempty read or writer mutation.

Per-chunk CSP closure is completed before that chunk's reader.  Later generated
invariant failure indicates violation of the declared unchanged FST/BAL
lifecycle or an internal error; because time-to-first-result is prioritized,
RHE does not promise transaction rollback of previously written chunks.
External reader/writer failures stop execution immediately and may likewise
leave a completed or externally partial prefix under STO-003.  Callbacks may not
retain reusable workspace views.

Wrong types/dtypes raise `TypeError`; invalid supported request values/shapes/
aliases raise `ValueError`; fixed-width byte/index arithmetic raises
`OverflowError`.  Inputs and reader state are not mutated by RHE.  Writer state
is the only external output.

## Memory, Evidence, And Performance

Let `S=slot_capacity`, `V=product(padded)`.  The capacity-dependent raw managed
arrays are exactly:

```text
selected IDs + payload + REL + RSL + RPH
= S * (8*F*V + 1854) bytes.
```

One reusable CWA payload of shape `(1,F,Bx+1,By+1,Bz+1)`, one exact 18-row
4,500-byte CSP plan, two `(26,3)` TGT arrays, at-most-four-row FRP scratch,
one-row SLB/CWP scratch, at-most-26-row primary PWA metadata, derived triplets,
and local writer-field IDs are also managed.  `managed_array_bytes` is exactly
the sum of `.nbytes` for every executor-owned ndarray in these families plus
the capacity-dependent arrays above; array headers are excluded.  Borrowed
forest/adapter state, page cache, writer output, and process RSS are reported
separately.  No complete field payload, REL table, or halo result is retained.

`RefinedHaloExecutionStats` is an immutable named tuple with exact Python-int
fields `primary_count`, `chunk_count`, `reader_calls`, `writer_calls`,
`selected_load_count`, `maximum_selected_slots`, and `managed_array_bytes`, in
that order.  `primary_count=P`; `chunk_count` is the number of nonempty SPR
chunks; `reader_calls==writer_calls==chunk_count` and excludes the one empty
adapter-conformance call of each kind; `selected_load_count` is the sum of
chunk selected counts including repeated support; `maximum_selected_slots` is
their maximum or zero for no chunks; and `managed_array_bytes` has the exact
raw-array meaning above.  Evidence covers sparse gaps, all kinds/phases/masks, CWP
closure before reader, no unselected-primary reads/writes, capacity invariance,
support/interior preservation, adapter failures, resident reference, safe
current comparison, and exact PWA/nonfinite behavior.

RHE is composition-only.  The group standard run records planning/read/SAME/
RST/CWA/PRL/PWA/write stages, time to first completed chunk, requested/read/
output bytes, support amplification, kind/tile/cell counts, managed memory and
RSS, small/medium/full selections, and bounded/full-capacity equivalence.  PWA
receives its separate hot-kernel measurements in the same run.

The first standard profile classified repeated per-action standalone validation
as a material composition bottleneck.  The retained optimized preflight must be
bitwise/statistically equivalent to the checked reference, may not increase
managed bytes or change reader/writer calls, must improve medium preflight by at
least 20%, and may not regress small time to first write by more than 10%.

## Immediate Consumer

The completed primary workspace is the required boundary before extracting the
refined locator/repeated point sampler.  Native selective `.dat` and selected
local-field executors reuse this reader/chunk/halo composition without changing
its numerical contracts.  SAM-002/SAM-003 remain fused compatibility paths.
