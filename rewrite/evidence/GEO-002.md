# GEO-002 Evidence

## Correctness And Numerical Boundary

GEO-002 maps explicit canonical FST leaf IDs to selected Cartesian 3D bounds
and slot-aligned cell spacing.  It consumes an unchanged FST-002 lifecycle but
does not rescan forest semantics or depend on topology, balance, contact caches,
cell centers, sampling ownership, payload, storage, or scheduling.  Independent
contract review classified the one affine/dyadic transform Green on all five
decomposition questions.

Nine focused tests and all 277 accumulated rewrite tests pass.  Level-one
output is bitwise identical to GEO-001, including repeated/reordered/empty
selections.  Mixed levels agree with an independent exact-`Fraction` reference
within the contracted bounds.  Same-level and coarse/fine faces are bitwise
identical, and every outer face preserves the supplied endpoint bits.

Tests cover the maximum FST level 63, refined effective-cell overflow,
level-created subnormal spacing, base subnormal spacing, selected large-origin
bound collapse, late invalid IDs, dtype/shape/layout/writability failures, and
output/output and output/input overlap.  All failures leave both outputs
unchanged.  Cell-center representability is deliberately not asserted here;
it remains a separate refined owner/sampling decision.

The representative WENO metadata reconstructs 25,844 nodes and 22,614 leaves
at levels three through six, with exact agreement to stored levels and one-based
coordinates.  Geometry spacing is bitwise equal to current `rnode`, while
canonical/current bounds differ by at most `3.33e-16`, within the contract
tolerance.  The staggered header limits this to geometry metadata evidence.

## Shared-Face And Current Evidence

Every real directed face is expanded to the exact covering leaf set using
TOP-002.  GEO-002 has zero bit mismatches across all 140,870 comparisons:

| Relation | Comparisons | GEO-002 mismatches | Current `rnode` mismatches |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| same level | 117,894 | 0 | 81,550 |
| fine to coarse | 11,488 | 0 | 8,396 |
| coarse to fine children | 11,488 | 0 | 8,396 |
| total | 140,870 | 0 | 98,342 |

Current maximum shared-face gap is `3.33e-16`.  Current forms upper faces from
a rounded lower face plus a rounded width; GEO-002 instead derives both sides
from one global level-cell face identity and snaps endpoints.  The difference
is intentional canonicalization rather than unsupported numerical behavior.

## Runtime, Scaling, And Memory

Final Apple arm64 measurements use 15 repetitions and permuted full selections.
The checked column includes Python layout/alias checks plus compiled selected
validation and fill; the lifecycle also runs FST-002 conformance.

| Max level | Leaves | Checked | Mleaf/s | Raw fill | FST->GEO lifecycle | Output bytes | Fraction reference |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 1 | 25.67 us | 0.04 | 1.33 us | 30.46 us | 72 | 45.88 us |
| 2 | 8 | 24.33 us | 0.33 | 1.25 us | 30.83 us | 576 | 0.117 ms |
| 3 | 64 | 24.79 us | 2.58 | 1.75 us | 32.12 us | 4,608 | 0.717 ms |
| 4 | 512 | 39.25 us | 13.04 | 6.54 us | 49.37 us | 36,864 | 6.28 ms |
| 5 | 4,096 | 0.167 ms | 24.56 | 0.059 ms | 0.201 ms | 294,912 | not run |

On the real tree, checked geometry takes `0.621 ms` at `36.44 million
leaves/s`; selected validation takes `0.350 ms`, raw fill `0.220 ms`, and the
FST-to-geometry lifecycle `0.777 ms`.  Allocating output takes `0.594 ms`.
The current eager `rnode` constructor over an already built forest takes
`0.284 ms` but performs no comparable conformance/atomic validation; copying
its already retained table takes `0.028 ms`.

The production call observes zero retained traced bytes and a fixed
2,256--2,288-byte traced peak from one through 4,096 leaves.  Caller output is
exactly `72*S` bytes.  A complete real output is 1,628,208 bytes; traversing it
in 4,096-leaf selections takes `0.713 ms` with one reused 294,912-byte geometry
buffer.  No selected-size scratch, full implicit table, or center array is
retained.

The two compiled passes are required by the stable atomicity contract.  Raw
fill is already faster than current eager materialization, and caching would
exchange a sub-millisecond selected computation for retained state.  No further
credible variant materially improves the runtime-memory-complexity trade-off,
so optimization stops at the direct validation/fill implementation.
