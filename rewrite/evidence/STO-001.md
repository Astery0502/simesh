# STO-001 Evidence

## Correctness

Eight focused tests and all 62 accumulated rewrite tests pass. Gather and
scatter agree bitwise with independent basic-slice/`np.copyto` references for
translated asymmetric boxes, reordered and duplicate block/field selections,
empty requests, and all three row/plane/slab copy paths. Binary64 payload tests
preserve positive and negative zero, a chosen NaN payload, and infinity exactly.

Scatter's slot-major/field-slot duplicate order is frozen by an exact last-write
test. Late invalid selectors, translated overflow, output/input overlap,
scatter self-overlap, bounds, shape, dtype, and writability errors leave outputs
unchanged. The current level-1 SFC block surface feeds backing storage directly;
arbitrary block/field gathers and a scatter roundtrip agree exactly.

Independent review ran 5,000 randomized valid gather/scatter cases against the
reference with bitwise agreement and found no remaining layering, pointer,
atomicity, or benchmark issue.

## Runtime, Throughput, And Allocation

Full-field measurements transfer 96 selected block/field pairs. Payload GB/s
counts bytes once; estimated memory traffic is twice these values.

| Block cells | Gather payload GB/s | Scatter payload GB/s | Traffic range GB/s |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `8^3` | 14.7--17.7 | 12.9--14.0 | 25.8--35.5 |
| `16^3` | 27.3--37.8 | 31.8--33.9 | 54.5--75.7 |
| `32^3` | 18.4--34.3 | 24.5--35.3 | 36.7--70.6 |

Ranges cover ordered, coprime-permuted, and duplicate selectors. For fixed
50,331,648-byte transfers, checked gather/scatter took 2.35/3.37 ms for 4,096
`8^3` blocks, 2.12/1.78 ms for 512 `16^3` blocks, and 1.81/1.86 ms for 64
`32^3` blocks. Small one-block calls show the fixed 15--36 us validation/Python
boundary before transfers become bandwidth-bound.

For 32 selected `8^3` blocks and three fields:

| Path | Gather time | Scatter time | Gather payload GB/s |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Full contiguous slab | 26.7 us | 28.9 us | 14.7 |
| Padded, z-full plane | 31.4 us | 28.5 us | 12.5 |
| Padded general rows | 48.9 us | 39.9 us | 8.0 |

The contiguous-suffix optimization reduced the initial ordered `8^3`
gather/scatter from 55.4/54.2 us to 26.7/28.9 us (2.1x/1.9x), and `16^3`
from 190/175 us to 105/97.7 us (1.8x each). At `32^3`, memory bandwidth and
selection locality dominate; selector sorting/run grouping is therefore
deferred to composed STO-002 planning rather than added here.

`tracemalloc` reports zero retained allocation and a fixed 1,536-byte peak for
every mode and selection size through 4,096 blocks. All payloads are
caller-preallocated, and the Cython kernels allocate no payload or
selection-sized scratch.
