# FND-001 Evidence

## Environment

- Apple M1, arm64, macOS 26.5.2
- CPython 3.11.14
- NumPy 2.4.4
- Cython 3.2.4

These measurements describe this machine; they are not contractual thresholds.

## Correctness

The focused and accumulated rewrite suites both passed with 12 tests. They
establish exact agreement between the NumPy reference and Cython path for
ravel/unravel, asymmetric interior bounds, and region copies. Axis-coded arrays
verify the full `(slot, field, x, y, z)` offset. NaNs outside the source region
verify that the copy does not read invalid cells. Read-only inputs, zero extents,
non-overlap, mutation boundaries, dtype, rank, contiguity, bounds, and signed
`int64` representability are covered.

The current-implementation comparison constructs a synthetic level-1 dataset
through `simesh.amrvac.load_from_uniform`. Its eight blocks are exactly
`float64`, C-contiguous, and shaped `(8, 2, 2, 4, 4)`. Packing those blocks into
an asymmetrically padded rewrite payload and extracting them with the independent
reference reproduces every value exactly; field/x/y/z increments of
`1/1000/100/10` independently prove the axis meanings.

## Runtime, Throughput, And Allocation

The retained benchmark copies 4 slots by 3 fields between asymmetric padded
buffers. Payload throughput counts bytes copied once; traffic throughput counts
one read plus one write.

| Cubic extent | Cells copied | Median time | Payload GB/s | Traffic GB/s |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 8 | 6,144 | 17.3 us | 2.84 | 5.67 |
| 16 | 49,152 | 41.0 us | 9.60 | 19.20 |
| 32 | 393,216 | 145.3 us | 21.66 | 43.31 |
| 48 | 1,327,104 | 454.5 us | 23.36 | 46.72 |

At extent 48, the source and destination allocations are 12,734,496 and
12,984,192 bytes. `tracemalloc` reports zero retained bytes and a 1,293-byte
peak increase during the validated call. Inspection and the fixed peak across
all four sizes establish that the kernel has no payload-sized temporary; memory
scales only with caller-owned source and destination buffers.

The initial scalar Cython loop took 3.814 ms at extent 48 (5.57 GB/s traffic).
Replacing only the contiguous z loop with row `memcpy` reduced this to 0.455 ms
(46.72 GB/s at the final 31-repeat measurement), an 8.4x improvement, while
preserving all exact tests. The NumPy reference took 0.563 ms (37.69 GB/s) for
the same extent. Further tiling or
parallelism would add complexity to an already memory-bandwidth-scale primitive;
there is no credible FND-001 variant left with a material trade-off improvement.

Benchmark command:

```text
PYTHONPATH=rewrite/src .venv/bin/python rewrite/benchmarks/fnd_001.py --repeats 31 --warmups 5
```
