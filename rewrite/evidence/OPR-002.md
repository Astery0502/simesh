# OPR-002 Evidence

## Correctness, Reach, And Composition

Thirteen focused tests and all 138 accumulated rewrite tests pass. Exact
coverage includes translated source/output regions and fields, every axis,
operation-order bit discrimination, axis-only reach, transverse-edge outputs,
one-layer sufficiency, source-center nonaccess, output preservation, empty
requests, and atomic spacing/axis/field/region/support/output/readonly/overlap/
overflow failures.

The arithmetic is separately rounded inverse spacing, plus-minus subtraction,
then multiply. The retained discriminator produces bits `0xd6b1297fe19bd436`;
rewriting as `difference/(2*spacing)` produces the adjacent `...435` result.
Independent audit ran 1,200 randomized finite translated-region cases over all
axes and found bitwise NumPy equality with no kernel, reach, region, atomicity,
allocation, or composition defect.

Tight reach is one lower/upper cell only on the chosen axis and zero
transversely. Tests compute transverse-edge cells that current's conservative
`[1:-1]^3` loop leaves at initialized zero. Both neighbors are always read;
the center value may be NaN without affecting a finite result.

Current one-term coefficient-one affine derivatives agree bitwise over its
common valid box. The current batching layer changes a direct `-0.0` derivative
to `+0.0` through `+0 + 1*contribution`; OPR-002 intentionally preserves the
direct stencil sign. Same-sign infinities yield NaN, opposite infinities yield
signed infinity, finite subtraction may overflow, and NaNs propagate according
to the fixed tree.

Affine x/y/z derivatives reproduce `1`, `2`, and `5`; continuous physical halos
produce the expected half slopes on only the outer physical cell planes. A
smooth sine sequence meets the retained second-order L-infinity floor of 1.8.
The bounded full-halo plan/gather/HAL/derivative/scatter path is bitwise equal to
an independent full-resident result with reordered fields.
