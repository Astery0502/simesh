# INT-001 Evidence

## M0 End-To-End Correctness

Five focused INT tests and all 154 accumulated rewrite tests pass. The focused
suite compares pointwise backing, stencil backing, native placement, zero-order
grid, trilinear grid, and reduction with an independently composed full-resident
reference. A read-only memmap uses reordered/repeated local fields and distinct
boundary rows; capacities 27 and 64 produce bit-identical final artifacts and
reduction, fully overwrite sentinel outputs, preserve the source, and exercise
both planner passes.

Late corruption of the final Morton row or face relation is rejected before any
of the five outputs changes. Tests cover every result-result pair and result-
backing/metadata overlap, budget 0/7/state-only/below-minimum/exact/clamped/type/
negative/overflow boundaries, and safe current native/zero/trilinear comparison.

Independent audit verified complete semantic preflight ordering: zero-slot fixed
checks, allocation-free full MOR/TOP proof, closure/capacity sizing, exact raw
workspace allocation, actual-ID dry traversal of both plans, then result
mutation. Profiling separates preflight, pass one, and pass two. No remaining
correctness, atomicity, boundedness, overwrite, numerical, accounting, or
performance finding remains.

## Budget And Capacity Scaling

The retained benchmark uses a read-only 4,718,592-byte memmap with an `8x6x4`
root, `8^3` blocks, six backing fields, and local fields `[4,1,5]`. Per-slot raw
managed bytes are exactly 28,104. Five caller-owned outputs total 5,922,816
bytes and are outside the temporary budget.

| Capacity | Managed bytes | No-closure chunks | Halo chunks | Halo amplification | Cold | Warm |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 27 | 758,816 | 8 | 76 | 10.250 | 61.41 ms | 57.02 ms |
| 64 | 1,798,664 | 3 | 10 | 3.156 | 17.78 ms | 17.60 ms |
| 128 | 3,597,320 | 2 | 3 | 1.781 | 12.39 ms | 12.14 ms |
| 192 | 5,395,976 | 1 | 1 | 1.000 | 13.49 ms | 10.37 ms |

Every requested/returned capacity and actual `.nbytes` sum equals
`8 + capacity*28,104`; every artifact comparison and reduction-bit comparison
is true. Capacity 27 proves the mapped source is 6.2x larger than managed raw
workspace. Capacity 128 keeps source larger than workspace and is within about
17% of the full-capacity warm runtime. Capacity 192 is the maximum possible, so
no larger variant remains to explore.

Traced peaks are 781,683; 1,807,259; 3,605,339; and 5,403,963 bytes for
capacities 27/64/128/192—raw workspace plus small fixed/control overhead.
Retained traced deltas fall from 15,712 to 832 bytes as chunk calls decrease.
The first cold mapped run incurs 25 major/616 minor faults; warm subsequent runs
have zero major faults. Raw process peak RSS is reported (about 51--67 MB), not
claimed as bounded: outputs, mapping/page cache, topology, interpreter, and
fixed control allocations are outside `budget_bytes` by contract.

## Stage Profile And Trade-Off

At capacity 64, one profiled 17.56 ms call divides into 1.14 ms preflight,
1.91 ms pass one, and 14.51 ms pass two. Dominant pass-two stages are physical
halos (6.13 ms), sibling halos (3.36 ms), trilinear sampling (3.45 ms), and
gather (0.78 ms). Pointwise, placement, zero sampling, reduction, derivative,
and both scatters are individually sub-millisecond.

At capacity 128, pass two falls to 9.28 ms; at full capacity it is 6.91 ms.
These measurements explain the nonlinearity: larger chunks reduce repeated
support loads and physical-envelope work, while the established simple kernels
remain individually useful. Further fusion would duplicate semantics for a
smaller remaining gain and is not retained.

## Milestone Result

M0 now provides one coherent non-periodic Cartesian 3D level-1 path with exact
Morton/topology/placement, explicit geometry/storage/budgets, physical and
sibling halos, zero/trilinear sampling, pointwise/stencil operators, streaming
reduction, current/reference comparisons, and a complete read-only mapped
bounded executor. Known current divergences are bounded lower-contract choices
(noncommuting mixed HAL order, unsafe sampling face windows, collapsed grids,
and stencil batching signed zero), not unresolved M0 work.
