# HAL-002 Evidence

## Correctness And Integration

Eight focused tests and all 87 accumulated rewrite tests pass. The retained
tests cover asymmetric lower/upper halos, direct faces, edges, corners,
singleton root axes, shuffled global-ID/slot mappings, NaN-filled support halos,
zero-primary/end cases, and exact preservation of primary interiors, pure
physical cells, and every support slot. Missing closure, duplicate IDs, excess
depth, read-only payloads, and overlap are rejected before mutation.

The bounded composition plans a full-halo STO-002 chunk, gathers selected
interiors with STO-001, applies HAL-001, and completes only the primary prefix
with HAL-002. The primary padded box is finite and bitwise equal to an
independent direct-cell reference even though support IDs are reordered and
support halo bits remain unspecified. This exercises both immediate producers
and establishes the full valid region needed by SAM-003/OPR-002.

Independent implementation audit sampled 500 cases over random `1..4 x 1..4 x
1..3` topologies, shuffled unique slot maps, zero through all primaries, one to
three fields, asymmetric zero-to-full-block halo widths, and random boundary
modes. Every result agreed bitwise with a coordinate oracle. A separate probe
preserved signaling/quiet NaN payloads, signed zero, and infinities exactly.
The audit found no correctness, atomicity, memory, or performance defect.

## Current Comparison And Intentional Difference

A full `2^3` current level-1 mesh with continuous, symmetric, and antisymmetric
modes agrees bitwise over every block, field, interior, face, edge, and corner.
The exact sibling placement also agrees across independently probed `2^3`,
`3^3`, and `4x3x2` roots.

Independent contract review exposed a current pass-order dependency when
no-inflow and antisymmetry meet at a mixed physical/sibling corner. The retained
fixture has x-low no-inflow, y-high antisymmetry, and a z-high sibling crossing.
For primary ID 2, the eight cells `[x=0:2,y=6:8,z=6:8]` are exact `-0.0` under
HAL-001's fixed x/y/z composition and exact `-611.0` in the current kernel; all
other bits agree. A second review fixture found the same ordering cause over 16
cells. HAL-002 intentionally keeps the deterministic HAL-001 order, and the
corrected contract was independently re-reviewed and accepted.

## Decomposition Addendum

The Phase A audit classified HAL-002 as semantically correct but Red because
relation/source-slot planning, same-level transfer, and HAL-001 physical
post-transforms were fused.  HPL-001 now exposes the first decision as exact
caller-owned plan arrays and proves effective per-target equivalence through an
independent consumer.  A plan-consuming rewrite of the optimized cell loop was
rejected after multiple broad-halo kernel regressions exceeded 20%; the stable
HAL-002 entrypoint therefore retains its faster sibling-only stack lookup.

This preserves every existing result and performance baseline while making the
new relation plan available to the next explicit transfer boundary.  HAL-002
now also consumes PBC-001's independently tested physical coordinate/value
rules inline.  It remains Red only until the pure HAX-001 plan-consuming
same-level transfer is independently available; no refined mode is added to it.
