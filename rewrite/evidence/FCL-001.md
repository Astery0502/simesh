## Runtime, Amplification, And Memory

Final Apple arm64 measurements traverse the retained `32^3` topology 15 times:

| Capacity | Chunks | Amplification | Utilization | Canonical Mprimary/s | Wrapper Mprimary/s | Buffer bytes |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |

| 1,024 | 55 | 1.702 | 99.0% | 0.935 | 0.936 | 8,192 |

Chunk counts, selected/primary amplification, utilization, promotion/order, and
suffix behavior are identical to the retained baseline.  Runtime remains within
ordinary run variance of 3.86/2.24/0.95 Mprimary/s.  The canonical and wrapper
paths are effectively equal because the closure's bounded linear membership
work dominates fixed validation.

The minimum helper scans 32,768 rows in 35.63 us at 919.8 million blocks/s; the
compatibility helper takes 35.00 us and returns the same exact minimum seven.
For a 192-block reference case, production is 3.79 us versus 61.21 us for the
list oracle.

One checked plan retains zero traced bytes and has a fixed 576--600-byte traced
peak.  The existing O(1) primary-interval recognition plus support-only linear
scan remains the best measured simple policy; a hash, bitmap, sort, or global
marker would add state and destabilize order without a credible gain.  No
further variant materially improves the runtime-memory-complexity trade-off.
