# MOR-001 Evidence

## Correctness

Sixteen focused tests and all 36 accumulated rewrite tests pass. The clipped
recursive Cython traversal agrees exactly with the arbitrary-precision sorted
reference for every one of the 512 positive shapes with extents from one
through eight. Exact hard-coded cube and clipped maps freeze x/y/z bit order,
dense-gap removal, inverse consistency, and C-array layout distinctions.

Nine retained current-implementation comparisons cover cubes, asymmetric
rectangles, unequal power-of-two sides, and singleton axes. Separate recovery
and review probes compared all 512 small shapes with the current implementation
and random shapes through power-of-two boundaries with the independent
reference. All ranks and coordinates agree exactly. A `(2049, 1, 1)` case also
proves that the rewrite does not inherit the current ten-bit coordinate cap.
The reference key is tested beyond 64 bits and rejects negative coordinates.

Caller-owned fill tests establish full overwrite, input preservation, exact
TOP-style coordinate/rank lookup, and pre-mutation rejection of wrong dtype,
shape, contiguity, writability, overlap, nonpositive extents, and volume
overflow. Independent review found no remaining pointer-safety or contract
issue.

## Runtime, Throughput, And Memory

Final Apple M1 measurements used 15 rewrite repetitions. Current timings used
three repetitions and exclude allocation in both implementations.

| Shape | Coordinates | Rewrite time | Mcoord/s | Output bytes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `(16,16,16)` | 4,096 | 18.6 us | 219.9 | 131,072 |
| `(32,32,32)` | 32,768 | 118.2 us | 277.2 | 1,048,576 |
| `(33,33,33)` | 35,937 | 129.9 us | 276.7 | 1,149,984 |
| `(64,64,64)` | 262,144 | 847.0 us | 309.5 | 8,388,608 |
| `(31,29,27)` | 24,273 | 89.1 us | 272.5 | 776,736 |
| `(127,113,97)` | 1,392,047 | 5.027 ms | 276.9 | 44,545,504 |
| `(65536,1,1)` | 65,536 | 839.2 us | 78.1 | 2,097,152 |

Balanced and clipped grids scale linearly at roughly 220--310 million
coordinates/s. The long singleton-axis case visits more occupied prefixes per
coordinate but still scans no enclosing cube.

`tracemalloc` reports zero retained fill allocation for all but the smallest
measurement artifact and a fixed 1,053-byte peak increase at every size. The
two required `int64` outputs occupy exactly `32*N` bytes; the kernel has no heap
scratch or per-coordinate allocation.

| Shape | Current time | Rewrite time | Speedup |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `(32,32,32)` | 2.028 ms | 118.2 us | 17.2x |
| `(31,29,27)` | 9.010 ms | 89.1 us | 101x |
| `(33,33,33)` | 2.387 s | 129.9 us | 18,400x |
| `(64,64,64)` | 16.84 ms | 847.0 us | 19.9x |

The current threshold collapse comes from enclosing-cube allocation and suffix
compaction, not different semantics. A validated memoryview-recursive rewrite
baseline took 13.23 ms for `(127,113,97)`; using raw pointers only inside the
same checked contract reduced this to 5.027 ms, a 2.6x improvement. A temporary
raw-code scan was modestly faster on a full power-of-two cube but became slower
immediately above the boundary and arbitrarily poor for slabs, so it was not
retained. Further strategy selection would worsen complexity for a one-time map.

```text
PYTHONPATH=rewrite/src:src .venv/bin/python rewrite/benchmarks/mor_001.py --repeats 15 --reference-limit 50000 --current-shapes 32x32x32,31x29x27,33x33x33,64x64x64 --current-repeats 3
```
