# BAL-001 Evidence

## Correctness And Boundary

BAL-001 owns only the global Cartesian 3D all-touch two-to-one policy.  It
reuses TOP-002 contact semantics without retaining contacts and consumes one
unchanged FST-002 artifact lifecycle.  It does not repeat forest conformance,
assign relation kinds, materialize topology, or plan support and value transfer.

Thirty-six focused tests and all 268 accumulated rewrite tests pass.  Uniform and
one-level mixed trees are accepted.  Deterministic contact/away child phases
are covered in all 26 directions.  Face level gaps of two are rejected, as
is a 32-leaf fixture whose six-face gaps are all at most one but whose diagonal
edge/corner gap is two.  A deeper child away from a coarse source's contact is
accepted, freezing the exact four/two/one touching-child selection for faces,
edges, and corners.

One hundred deterministic randomized complete octrees agree with an
independent pairwise reference.  The reference lifts leaf boxes to a common
maximum-level integer lattice and compares every closed-box touching pair; it
does not call TOP-002 or follow production child pointers.  Tests also freeze
physical directions, deterministic first-violation reporting, invalid
shape/dtype behavior, and level-63 shifts.  A separate 500-forest development
probe found no production/reference disagreement.

The representative WENO tree has 25,844 nodes, 22,614 leaves, and maximum
level six.  BAL-001 accepts all 587,964 leaf directions.  Its staggered header
keeps this as forest/topology metadata evidence rather than payload support.

## Runtime, Scaling, And Memory

Final Apple arm64 measurements use 15 repetitions.  Throughput counts all 26
noncenter directions per leaf; the lifecycle column includes FST-002
conformance immediately followed by BAL-001.

The face-balanced diagonal violation is detected in 6.58 us.  The real tree
validates in 7.04 ms at 83.58 million leaf-directions/s; the actual FST->BAL
lifecycle is 7.21 ms.  The median descriptive current `AMRForest` construction
takes 17.34 ms on the same run, although it constructs and retains materially
more topology and is not a semantic kernel comparator.

The checked operation retains no output or leaf-sized scratch.  The level-five
probe observes a fixed 64-byte traced current delta and 1,544-byte traced peak.
Runtime scales with leaf-directions and contact depth; the quadratic reference
is deliberately limited to small cases.  No cache variant is credible for a
once-per-artifact policy check, so optimization stops at the direct shared
inline contact traversal.

## Composition Decision

FST-002, TOP-002, and BAL-001 now form separate conformance, contact, and
admissibility boundaries.  Optional TOP-003 remains deferred: no immediate
consumer has yet shown that retaining `54*L` bytes of six-face relations beats
on-demand contact use in the runtime-memory-complexity trade-off.  A later
relation consumer may reopen that choice with composed evidence; refined
geometry proceeds without a balance or cache dependency.
