## Kernel Optimization And Scaling

The retained kernel originally walked 26 directions once to test a candidate
and again to commit accepted support.  After correctness was frozen, HCL keeps
at most 26 newly discovered IDs on a fixed C stack and commits them without the
second walk.  This preserves order/union exactly, adds no size-dependent state,
and removes the only credible redundant work.

Exact chunks, amplification, utilization, primary/support order, and suffix

The minimum helper returns exact 27 and scans 32,768 rows in 2.431 ms at 13.48
million blocks/s; its wrapper takes 2.425 ms.  For a 120-block reference case,
production takes 6.29 us versus 336.37 us for the list oracle.  One checked
plan retains zero traced bytes and has a fixed 576--600-byte traced peak.
