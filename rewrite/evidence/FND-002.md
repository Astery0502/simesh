# FND-002 Evidence

## Correctness

Eight focused tests and all 20 accumulated rewrite tests pass. The compiled
region arithmetic agrees exactly with an independent Python/NumPy reference
across 100 deterministic randomized asymmetric boxes, zero reach, canonical
empty boxes, each one-cell-short face, and signed-`int64` boundary cases.
Inputs remain unchanged. An independent review also probed mixed underflow and
overflow precedence and found no remaining issue after the regression fix.

The current-implementation comparison constructs a level-1 `AMRMesh` directly,
fills its full padded source with `i + 10*j + 100*k`, and evaluates current
central derivatives along all three axes. FND-002 predicts the exact valid box
`[1, 7)^3` from source-valid `[0, 8)^3` and one-cell reach. The current output is
exactly `444` throughout that box and zero on all six excluded outer faces.

## Runtime, Memory, And Reach Volume

On the Apple M1 environment recorded for FND-001, 900,000 calls per primitive
(100,000 iterations by nine repetitions) gave:

| Primitive | Median validated call |
| --- | ---: |
| Required-input expansion | 11.97 us |
| Valid-output contraction | 11.40 us |

One expansion returns 48 bytes of index payload. `tracemalloc` reports a
384-byte retained increase while those results remain live and a 1,440-byte
peak increase. Cost is fixed `O(3)`, independent of field payload size, with no
payload access. It is orchestration metadata rather than a cell-loop kernel, so
payload throughput and parallel scaling do not apply.

For output extent `(32, 48, 64)`, lower reach `(2, 0, 3)`, and upper reach
`(0, 4, 1)`, the exact required box contains 120,224 cells. Replacing it with a
symmetric per-axis reach would request 141,120 cells, 14.8% more. The asymmetric
triplets therefore preserve a material memory/I/O distinction without an
offset-list or executor abstraction.

The boundary validation dominates the small call and occurs once per planned
region, not per cell. Caller-supplied metadata outputs or cached plans are not
introduced until composed executor measurements show a need.
