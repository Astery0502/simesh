# CWP-001 Design: COARSER Explicit Workspace Placement And Reach

## Choice And Decomposition

CWP-001 maps one compact accepted COARSER row to a normalized coarse workspace
for PRL-001.  The workspace contains the exact coarse-center region required by
the fine target plus one axial slope cell on every side.  The relation's coarse
source interior is intersected with that required region and assigned an exact
workspace destination.  Uncovered workspace cells remain explicit work for
later refined support/PBC application.

Using source-block storage coordinates directly was rejected because required
PRL reach may be one cell below or above the source interior, including negative
logical coordinates.  Normalize by the lesser of the logical required lower
and logical PRL origin.  This keeps both the required region and shifted origin
nonnegative without changing PRL mapping.

Responsibility: derive normalized PRL workspace extent/origin and the relation
source's exact source/destination copy boxes for active COARSER rows.

Owned decisions: primary phase and direction coordinate mapping, required
center range, one-cell axial reach, normalization shift, source intersection,
and empty-row representation.

Explicitly non-owned decisions: relation/source identity, support closure for
uncovered cells, physical values, restriction/prolongation arithmetic, payload
allocation/copy, application order, cache, scheduling, periodicity, or 2D.

Inputs/outputs: one positive-even interior; reduced directions, COARSER
primary phases, and target boxes; seven caller-owned row-aligned box/origin arrays.

Mutation/ownership: borrowed inputs, full atomic output overwrite, no retention.

Access/halo reach: PRL's exact mapped centers plus one cell in every axis.

Reference/invariant: Python integer global-to-local mapping; PRL index
equivalence; source/destination extent equality and containment.

Producer/consumer: TGT/RPH produce rows; block-copy/support application fills
the workspace and PRL consumes its extent/origin.

Performance class: cold/control.  Work is exact `O(R)` integer geometry with
`168*R` caller-owned output bytes and no internal size-dependent allocation;
PRL mapping composition establishes usefulness without an artificial box-only
timing.

Five-question result: yes/yes/yes/yes/yes.

The first complete refined-halo consumer found that the initial checked
phase/sign gate encoded the current fine-to-coarse send schedule rather than
COARSER-to-fine prolongation geometry.  RPH explicitly permits either phase on
nonzero axes, and current `gc_prolong` applies every COARSER row.  Independent
integer, balanced-forest, WENO-metadata, and dyadic current comparisons approved
removing only that gate; the mapping formula, outputs, ownership, and all
previously accepted results remain unchanged.
