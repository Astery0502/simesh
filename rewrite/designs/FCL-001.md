# FCL-001 Design: Direct-Face Support Closure

## Trigger And Inventory

WSP-001 and PRI-001 extracted accounting and topology-free primary identity from
the STO-002 Yellow capability.  The remaining mode-bearing planner combines two
distinct policies: direct six-face closure and complete one-block halo closure.
FCL-001 extracts only the direct-face policy and its progress helper.

## Alternatives

Filling a complete PRI buffer and then adding support is not equivalent because
support reduces the primary count and discovered support can later be promoted.
A set/hash/bitmap would change stable order and add memory despite the bounded
six-face policy.  Generalizing to a closure callback would recreate the dynamic
policy system prohibited by the rewrite charter.

Retain the existing greedy caller-buffer algorithm: grow dense PRI candidates
one at a time, union their direct TOP faces, and commit only fitting trials.
Expose a mode-free canonical function and minimum-progress helper.  The old
`plan_level1_chunk(..., True, ...)` and `minimum_face_closed_slots(...)` paths
delegate after preserving legacy validation.

## Decomposition Record

Responsibility: plan the maximal dense primary prefix whose unique nonrecursive
direct-face support union fits explicit caller capacity.

Owned semantic decisions: face columns 0..5; physical sentinel skip; greedy
maximality; first-fit error; primary prefix; unique support; first-discovery
order; promotion and remaining-support order; suffix/end/atomicity; and exact
single-primary maximum/progress capacity.

Explicitly non-owned decisions: byte/capacity choice, topology construction or
per-call semantic rescan, full-halo edge/corner walks, refined closure, recursive
support, payload transfer, halo values, backend, caching, parallelism, or
scheduling.

Inputs and outputs: borrowed TOP-001 `int64[N,6]`, one first-primary scalar,
caller-owned writable `int64[S]`, two exact counts; the minimum helper returns
one exact count.

Mutation and ownership: inputs remain unchanged; first-fit errors occur before
mutation; success overwrites only selected prefix and preserves suffix; no
size-dependent scratch is retained.

Access pattern / halo reach: direct face metadata only; no diagonal or payload
access.

Independent reference or invariant: Python primary/support lists; exact union,
order, promotion, maximality, suffix, progress, and full traversal; preserved
STO wrapper/error conformance.

Immediate producer and consumer: PRI supplies dense candidate identity and TOP
supplies direct faces.  No numerical direct-face consumer remains; the stable
STO compatibility wrapper and face benchmark are the supported consumer/evidence.

Kernel and composed measurement boundary: primaries/s, selected IDs/s, chunk
count, load amplification, utilization, capacity scaling, minimum-helper rate,
fixed allocation, wrapper overhead, and small reference ratio.

Five-question result: yes/yes/yes/yes/yes.  Direct-face closure and its progress
minimum are one policy; full-halo and refined support stay independent.

## Compatibility Baseline

The retained `32^3` traversal produces 1,797/293/55 chunks, amplification
3.457/2.279/1.702, and approximately 3.86/2.24/0.95 million primaries/s for
capacities 64/256/1,024.  The current support-only linear scan already beat a
broader prefix scan by up to 2.9x, so no new indexing structure is justified.
