# CSP-001 Design: COARSER Slope-Support Planning

## Choice

Partition one nonempty CWP required rectangle on the primary-relative coarse
half-block lattice.  For `H=B/2`, workspace coarse origin `O`, required interval
`R`, and `e` in `{-1,0,+1}`, each axis contributes

```text
J_e = R intersect [O + e*H, O + (e+1)*H).
```

Nonempty Cartesian products are disjoint and cover `R`.  Face, edge, and
corner COARSER rows produce at most 18, 12, and 8 raw boxes.  Raw boxes wholly
inside CWP's direct workspace-source box are merged into its one direct record,
leaving at most 15 records, but caller storage remains fixed at 18.

For a nonphysical tile, center uses the primary slot and SAME uses its RSL slot;
both are restricted.  COARSER uses its RSL slot directly.  A physical tile
uses no new source: clearing masked direction components identifies an already
planned nonphysical base.  The plan carries the true logical interior and
storage-to-logical offset required by PWA, rather than pretending that the
clipped base is a block interior.

Recursive secondary closure was rejected under the selected `B>=4` and
`side_reach<=B/2` gate because exhaustive direction/phase probes and balanced
forest probes show no FINER or missing all-26 owner.  A seven-point-only plan
was rejected because it would change the current checked PRL/CWP valid-box
contract.  A 27-cell plan is valid but retains inactive directions and is not
needed once the exact maximum of 18 is established.

The complete RHE consumer also exposed a downstream accepted-domain mismatch:
CWP initially admitted only phase/sign rows used by the current restriction
send gate, while RPH and current prolongation admit every COARSER phase and
direction.  Independent expansion probes found no new owner, FINER tile, or
record-count increase.  CSP therefore validates side and reach but imposes no
phase/sign compatibility rule.

## Decomposition Record

Responsibility: map one explicit active CWP row to a bounded, complete,
source-resolved coarse-workspace support plan before payload I/O.

Owned decisions: half-block direction tiling; direct-box merge; center/SAME/
COARSER source scale; exact source and workspace boxes; physical base identity;
logical coordinate frame; complete coverage; fixed ordering and sentinels.

Explicitly non-owned decisions: primary selection, REL/RSL/RPH meaning, SPR
capacity/union, payload values, PBC transforms, RST/PRL arithmetic, workspace
allocation, reader/cache/backend, final halo order, periodicity, or 2D.

Inputs/outputs: one CWP row including its original fine target, one primary
slot/phase/reduced direction, aligned canonical all-26 REL/RSL rows, and
caller-owned 18-record plan arrays.

Mutation/ownership: complete preflight precedes full plan overwrite; inputs are
borrowed and nothing is retained.

Access/halo reach: exact CWP required rectangle; `B>=4`, each requested side no
larger than `B/2`; no leaf outside primary plus all-26 REL support is admitted.

Independent reference/invariant: Python-integer interval products, exact target
partition, local source mapping, source containment/ratio, and base ownership.

Immediate producer/consumer: CWP plus REL/RSL/RPH produce the plan; CWA consumes
it before PRL.

Performance class: cold/control, fixed at at most 18 records and `O(1)` work per
CWP row, with exact caller-owned bytes and no size-dependent internal scratch.

Five-question result: yes/yes/yes/yes/yes.  Support geometry is separate from
physical values and execution policy.  The consumed representation received an
independent material-contract review; the review found and resolved the 7-box,
fixed-8, `B=2`, and clipped-interior counterexamples before implementation.
