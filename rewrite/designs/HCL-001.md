# HCL-001 Design: Complete One-Block Halo Closure

## Trigger And Inventory

WSP-001, PRI-001, and FCL-001 extracted accounting, dense primary identity, and
direct-face support from STO-002.  The final independent decision is the
complete clipped one-block `3x3x3` closure used by level-1 halo consumers.

## Alternatives

Direct-face closure omits 20 diagonal interiors and is insufficient.  A generic
direction callback or refined mode would fuse HCL with REL/STO-004 policy.  A
hash/bitmap changes memory/order without evidence; recursive closure could cross
more than one block and exceed the contracted reach.

Retain the existing caller-buffered greedy algorithm.  Discover 26 noncenter
directions in x-fast order and walk faces x then y then z, clipping on physical
sentinels.  Expose a mode-free canonical function and derived minimum helper;
preserve legacy wrappers and migrate M0 full-halo preflight/pass two.

## Decomposition Record

Responsibility: plan the maximal dense primary prefix whose unique complete
clipped level-1 one-block `3x3x3` support union fits explicit capacity.

Owned semantic decisions: x-fast direction discovery; x/y/z face-walk order;
physical clipping; unique union; primary/support split; first-discovery support
order; promotion; greedy maximality/first-fit; suffix/end/atomicity; exactly-once
primary coverage; and exact single-primary maximum/progress capacity.

Explicitly non-owned decisions: byte/capacity choice, PRI identity, TOP
construction or per-call semantic repair, HPL relation masks/slots, halo width/
reach, physical/sibling values, transfer, payload/backend/cache/scheduling,
recursive/multiblock support, refined coarse/fine support, periodicity, or 2D.

Inputs and outputs: borrowed TOP-001 `int64[N,6]`, first-primary scalar,
caller-owned writable `int64[S]`, exact primary/selected counts; the minimum
helper returns one exact count.

Mutation and ownership: inputs unchanged; first-fit errors atomic; success
overwrites selected prefix only and preserves suffix; nothing retained and no
size-dependent scratch beyond the caller buffer.

Access pattern / halo reach: metadata closure exactly one logical block in each
axis; consumer halo widths and cell reach remain HAL/FND decisions.

Independent reference or invariant: Python x/y/z face walks and primary/support
lists; coordinate Chebyshev neighborhood; order/union/promotion/maximality;
minimum/progress; wrapper conformance; HPL sufficiency; INT artifact equality.

Immediate producer and consumer: PRI supplies candidates and TOP supplies face
steps.  M0 preflight/pass two is the first canonical consumer; HPL/HAL/HAX/SAM/
OPR remain compatibility evidence.  Refined support begins separately at
STO-004.

Kernel and composed measurement boundary: primaries/s, selected IDs/s, chunks,
amplification/utilization, capacity scaling, minimum-helper rate, fixed
allocation, wrapper/reference ratio, and INT pass-two runtime/artifacts.

Five-question result: yes/yes/yes/yes/yes.  The minimum is derived from the one
closure policy; all refined, value, storage, and execution decisions are split.

## Compatibility Baseline

The retained `32^3` traversal produces 3,952/454/64 chunks, amplification
7.371/3.517/1.983, utilization 95.5/99.2/99.2%, and about
0.914/0.417/0.205 million primaries/s for capacities 64/256/1,024.  The planner
is already small beside INT pass-two halo/sampling work; no new indexing
structure is justified without material composed improvement.
Canonical versus retained wrapper and INT pass two are measured in the same
run with exact IDs/artifacts as hard gates; regressions above 10% are
investigated and reproduced regressions above 20% block completion absent a
documented runtime-memory trade-off.
